# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, and publishing other people's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Report unacceptable behavior privately through the project's security reporting channel or by contacting a maintainer through GitHub.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
